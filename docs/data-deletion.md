## How to Delete Your Data from MarketingKart.ai

### Option 1: Remove App & Data via Facebook Settings (Instant)
If you connected your Facebook account, Pages, or Instagram via Facebook Login / Meta OAuth:

1. Log in to your Facebook account.
2. Go to **Settings & Privacy** ➔ **Settings**.
3. In the left-hand navigation menu, click **Apps and Websites**.
4. Search for **Marketingkart.ai** in the list of active apps.
5. Click **Remove** next to the app.
6. Check the box **"Delete posts, videos or events that Marketingkart.ai posted on your timeline"** and confirm.
7. Meta will automatically notify our servers to disconnect your tokens and purge linked campaign permissions.
